# Build complete autonomous systems — then race them

Micro Autonomy is where WATonomous members build and race 1:10 scale autonomous vehicles head-to-head. You integrate perception, localization, planning, and control into one racing machine, then watch your code compete on the track immediately.

## Why racing accelerates learning

This is not textbook robotics. The track can change mid-race, and opponents force you to adapt instantly. Members tackle hard questions under pressure:

- How do you localize when your map becomes outdated?
- When do you risk an overtake at maximum speed?
- How do you plan aggressive trajectories on limited compute without crashing?

Crashes are part of the process. Every race pushes the team to build smarter, faster systems.

## From campus to the world stage

F1Tenth gives what full-scale vehicles cannot always offer: rapid iteration, aggressive testing, and the freedom to fail fast. Members deploy algorithms in minutes, not months, and compete at conferences like IROS and ICRA.

The skills — real-time perception, motion planning, and control under constraints — are exactly what autonomy companies hire for.
