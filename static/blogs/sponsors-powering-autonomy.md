# Partnerships that keep robots rolling

WATonomous depends on sponsors who believe students can contribute meaningful work in robotics. Support ranges from sensors and compute to software tooling and career pathways for members.

## What sponsorship enables

- Hardware and sensors for EVE, Micro, Rover, and Humanoid
- Cloud and developer tooling used across software stacks
- Outreach, competitions, and team operations

## Become a sponsor

If your organization wants to support student-built autonomy at Waterloo, visit the [Sponsors](/sponsor) page and reach out through the sponsorship flow.

We are always excited to partner with companies that care about education, open collaboration, and the future of robotics.
