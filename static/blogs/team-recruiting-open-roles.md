# Students building autonomous robots

WATonomous’s mission is simple: show the world that a bunch of students can build autonomous robots. We are a project-based team at the University of Waterloo spanning full-scale vehicles, racing platforms, planetary rovers, and humanoids.

## Who we are looking for

We regularly open roles across software, electrical, mechanical, and business operations. Whether you want to work on LiDAR pipelines, race strategy, sponsor relations, or the website you are reading now — there is a place to learn by building.

## How to apply

1. Browse current openings on the [Careers / Get Involved](/careers) page
2. Read the posting carefully (responsibilities and qualifications)
3. Submit an application through the form on the posting

No prior autonomy experience is required for every role — curiosity, ownership, and a willingness to learn matter most.
