# Why EVE matters

EVE is WATonomous’s full-scale autonomous vehicle platform. Unlike tabletop robots, EVE must navigate complex intersections, interpret traffic signals, and predict unpredictable pedestrian behaviour — with the dynamics of a real vehicle on the line.

Students on the EVE project tackle challenges that smaller platforms cannot fully reproduce: dense urban traffic, emergency braking with vehicle dynamics, and automotive safety expectations. Every mistake has higher stakes, and that pressure is exactly what makes the work valuable.

## What we build

- **Perception** — LiDAR, cameras, and sensor fusion to understand the scene in real time
- **Localization & mapping** — knowing where the vehicle is, even when the environment changes
- **Planning** — generating safe trajectories through intersections and construction zones
- **Control** — turning planned paths into smooth, reliable vehicle commands

## Skills that transfer

Working on EVE prepares students for careers in autonomous transportation, logistics, and smart-city infrastructure. The algorithms and systems habits you build here map directly to the problems companies like Waymo, Aurora, and Nuro face every day.
