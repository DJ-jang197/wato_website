# A world-class autonomous rover

WATonomous partners with UWRT to develop autonomy for the University Rover Challenge (URC). The goal is a rover that can explore unknown environments using sensors to perceive its surroundings, localize itself, build maps, and identify science targets — without human intervention.

## The desert is not forgiving

Shifting sand, rocks, and uneven terrain force the rover to constantly update its maps and adjust its path. Low-contrast science targets that blend into the desert floor make reliable detection difficult.

To succeed, the team combines:

- Robust **perception** for terrain and science objects
- Reliable **localization** in GPS-challenged or visually confusing scenes
- Adaptive **planning** that keeps the rover safe and efficient in real time

## Broader impact

The same systems that help a rover finish URC missions also advance technologies useful for planetary exploration, disaster response, and field robotics on Earth.
